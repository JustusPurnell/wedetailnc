Detailing Built For
Lake Norman Living

Huntersville is lake town living with a short commute to Charlotte, and that means a specific set of car problems: hard water spots from the marina, tree sap and pollen from wooded lots, boat-trailer grime, and long driveways in gated communities off Vermillion and Antiquity. Our mobile setup is built to handle all of it right where your car is parked, without a trip into the city.

We provide mobile car detailing in Huntersville, NC for daily drivers, family SUVs, tow vehicles, and lake-house second cars. Whether it's mineral deposits from Lake Norman water, or you just want a full detail before a Birkdale Village weekend out, we bring the equipment to you.

Huntersville sits about 20 minutes north of Uptown Charlotte on I-77, and we run it as a full, regularly scheduled service area — not a once-in-a-while add-on trip.
