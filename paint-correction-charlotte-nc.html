What Paint Correction Targets

Improve the Defects That Make Paint Look Tired

Paint correction is designed to improve defects in the clear coat and restore a cleaner, glossier finish.

🌀

Swirl Marks

Machine polishing can significantly improve the circular marks commonly left by poor washing and automatic brush washes.

✦

Light Scratches

Shallow clear-coat scratches can often be reduced or removed depending on their depth.

☀️

Oxidation

Correction can improve dull or hazy paint caused by surface oxidation and environmental exposure.

✨

Lost Gloss

Polishing restores clarity and reflection so the paint looks deeper, cleaner and more refined.

What a Good Correction Should Change

The goal is not to simply make the car shiny for a day. Correction works the paint itself to improve defects and restore clarity before any protection is added.

  • ✓Fewer visible swirls under direct light
  • ✓Improved gloss and reflection
  • ✓Reduced shallow scratches
  • ✓Cleaner paint before coating or protection
Our Process

How We Correct Your Paint

Proper preparation comes first. The paint has to be clean and decontaminated before polishing can do its job correctly.

01

Prewash

Loose dirt, road film and contamination are removed before touching the paint.

02

Decontamination

The paint is cleaned and decontaminated so polishing is performed on a properly prepared surface.

03

Inspect the Paint

We evaluate the visible defects and choose the correction approach that fits the condition.

04

Machine Polish

The clear coat is polished to improve swirls, shallow scratches, oxidation and dullness.

05

Final Inspection

We review the finish and make sure the paint has improved before the job is complete.
