Justus
Purnell
Founder & Lead Detailer · Charlotte, NC

WeDetail Charlotte is a premium mobile detailing service serving Charlotte and surrounding areas. I built the business around a simple idea: make professional detailing easier by bringing the service directly to the customer.

I gained hands-on experience working around high-end vehicles at Rolls-Royce, where attention to detail and presentation standards matter. That same mindset carries into the way I approach every detail — whether it's an interior reset, paint correction or ceramic coating.

I'm also a Computer Science student at UNC Charlotte and built WeDetail while in school. What started as a mobile detailing business has grown through repeat customers, referrals and a reputation for consistent results across Charlotte.
