What Ceramic Coating Does

Long-Term Paint Protection With a Better Finish

Ceramic coating adds a durable protective layer over your paint to improve water behavior, make routine maintenance easier and keep the finish looking glossier for longer.

💧

Hydrophobic Protection

Water beads and rolls off the surface more easily, helping reduce the amount of grime that clings to the paint.

☀️

UV Resistance

Adds protection against environmental exposure that can contribute to fading and oxidation over time.

✨

Enhanced Gloss

Creates a deeper, slicker-looking finish that helps clean paint look richer and more reflective.

🧼

Easier Maintenance

A coated vehicle is generally easier to wash and maintain because contaminants have a harder time bonding to the surface.

Our Ceramic Coating Process

How We Prep and Coat Your Vehicle

Proper preparation matters. The coating is applied only after the exterior has been cleaned and decontaminated.

01

Prewash

We remove loose dirt and road grime before touching the paint.

02

Clay Bar

Bonded contaminants are removed so the paint surface is smoother and cleaner.

03

IPA Prep

The paint is wiped down to remove residue and prepare the surface for coating.

04

Coating Application

Professional-grade 5-year ceramic coating is applied across the exterior paint.

05

Final Inspection

We inspect the finish and make sure the application is complete before wrapping up.

Before You Coat

Does Your Paint Need Correction First?

Ceramic coating protects the condition your paint is already in. If the vehicle has visible swirls, scratches or oxidation, paint correction may be worth doing before the coating goes on.
